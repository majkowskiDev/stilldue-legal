Privacy Policy

How StillDue handles local commitment data, local notifications, purchases and visits to this website.

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

StillDue is a local-first commitment reminder app provided by Łukasz Majkowski in Poland. It does not require an account and does not use a StillDue backend or cloud sync.

Data stored on your device

Commitment titles, optional notes, due dates, outcomes, history, templates, settings and reminder tracking are stored locally in the app database on your device. StillDue does not upload this content to Łukasz Majkowski.

Local notifications

When reminders are enabled, StillDue gives the operating system the commitment title and reminder stage needed to display local notifications on your device. Notes are not included. StillDue does not use remote push notifications and does not claim exact or guaranteed delivery.

Purchases and RevenueCat

StillDue uses RevenueCat and the applicable app store to process and restore StillDue Pro purchases anonymously. RevenueCat receives purchase history and related store transaction information for app functionality and purchase analytics. Those services may also process store account, device and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. StillDue does not send commitment titles, notes or reminder content to RevenueCat.

App analytics and logging

StillDue does not include an app analytics SDK or advertising. The app is designed not to place commitment titles, notes or notification-visible private text in remote logs, billing attributes or analytics.

This website and GitHub Pages

The StillDue legal and support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The site code does not use analytics, advertising, forms or set cookies. GitHub may process standard request logs such as an IP address, browser details, requested page and request time to host and secure the service under GitHub's own privacy terms.

Privacy & choices

Notification permission is requested only after an in-app explanation. You can deny or revoke it at any time in system settings. StillDue continues to store and display commitments when notifications are unavailable.

Retention, backup and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them through available app controls or clear the app data. Removing the app normally removes its local database, but an operating system backup may include or restore app data depending on your device and backup settings. Purchase records are retained by the store and RevenueCat under their own policies.

Your requests

Because Łukasz Majkowski does not maintain an account or remote commitment database, local data is controlled through the app and device settings. For privacy questions, contact majkovski.dev@gmail.com.

Changes and applicable law

This policy may be updated as StillDue changes. Material updates will be reflected in the app and dated above. This policy is interpreted under applicable law, including mandatory consumer and data-protection rights.
