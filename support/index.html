Support

Help with commitments, reminders, local data and StillDue Pro purchases.

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Create a commitment

Add a title, optional note, future due date and intensity. StillDue shows the finite reminder plan before you save.

Notifications

StillDue asks for notification permission only after you create your first commitment and see an explanation. If reminders are off, use Settings to open the system settings. Focus, Do Not Disturb and device power management may delay or hide reminders.

DONE and SKIP

DONE records the commitment as completed. SKIP records that you chose not to complete it and resets the current streak. Either action asks StillDue to cancel future reminders.

Local data and backups

Your commitments, history and templates live in the local app database. StillDue has no account or cloud sync. Clearing app data or removing the app may remove records; an operating system backup may include or restore them depending on system settings.

StillDue Pro

StillDue Pro raises the active limit and adds full history, templates and advanced stats. Use Restore purchases if an eligible purchase is missing, or Manage subscription to open the store subscription settings.

Contact support
